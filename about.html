About NoorCare

A clinic built around one idea: a woman should never feel rushed, judged or unheard about her own health.

Women first, always

NoorCare opened in Gulberg, Lahore, as a small clinic with a simple standard: private rooms, appointments long enough for real questions, and explanations in plain Urdu or English.

We look after pregnancy, gynaecology, scans, hormonal health and life after birth — and we say plainly what we do not do: anything outside women's general health is referred on to the right specialist, with your notes.

No awards, no miracle claims — just steady, respectful care, visit after visit.
