Good to know before you visit

Honest answers to the questions every woman asks first.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes, absolutely. A mother, sister, husband or friend is welcome in the room if you want them there — or you can be seen alone. It is your choice.

Do you speak Urdu?

Yes. Consultations are available in Urdu and English, and reports are explained in plain words, never medical jargon.

How much will my visit cost?

Consultations start at Rs. 2,000 and every service price is listed openly on the services page — no hidden charges.

What should I bring?

Any previous reports, scans or medicine lists you have. If it is your first visit, just bring yourself — we start from scratch.
